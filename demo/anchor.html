Anchor placements

All nine tiles are anchored to the blue bar, not to themselves. Scroll the bar through the viewport and watch them fire. They land in three groups — when the anchor reaches the bottom of the viewport, its centre, then its top — because that second word is what sets the trigger line. The first word only changes how much of the anchor must be showing. They reset on the way back up.
